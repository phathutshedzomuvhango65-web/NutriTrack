Nutrition guide

A few basics to help you understand food and wellbeing.

🥚 Protein

Supports growth, muscle development and repair. Sources include eggs, beans, lentils, fish, chicken and yoghurt.

🍞 Carbohydrates

An important source of energy. Sources include bread, rice, potatoes, oats and maize meal.

🥑 Healthy fats

Help the body absorb certain vitamins and support normal body functions. Sources include avocado, nuts, seeds and vegetable oils.

🥦 Vitamins and minerals

Support many body functions, including immunity, bones and blood health. Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables.

🥛 Calcium

Helps build and maintain bones and teeth. Sources include milk, yoghurt and some fortified foods.

🍽️ Balanced eating

A varied eating pattern can include grains or starchy foods, protein foods, vegetables, fruit and healthy fats. There is no single perfect meal, and all foods can fit into a balanced lifestyle.
